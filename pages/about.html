Institutional Quality & Reliable Delivery

New Power Sports is an institutional uniform manufacturing and supply enterprise based in Tamil Nadu. Our core focus is outfitting educational and healthcare institutions with medical lab coats, nursing scrubs, patient care attendant wear, and workshop aprons.

We bridge the gap between quality fabric sourcing and institutional volume requirements. Whether providing an entire academic year's batch supply for medical admissions or outfitting healthcare hospital staff, we deliver consistent sizing, fade-resistant fabrics, and crisp computer logo embroidery.
